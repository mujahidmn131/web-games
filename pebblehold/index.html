Valley theme (unlocks by best wave)

A tower-defence roguelite: YOU lay the road the enemies must march. Longer, windier road = more time under your towers' fire.

How to Play

1. Draft the road. Before each wave you get 3 tiles. Tap a tile, then tap a glowing cell next to the road end to lay it. Straight keeps going the same way, Corner turns, Bridge crosses a rock. The road must always leave a way open to the Keep — a blocked placement is refused.
2. Build towers. Tap a tower card, then tap a grass cell beside the road. Tap a tower again to upgrade it (twice) or sell it back for 65%.
3. Hold the wave. Press START WAVE. Enemies march your road; towers fire on their own. Leaks cost Keep hearts — 0 hearts ends the run.
4. Draft a card. After each cleared wave pick 1 of 3 cards: a new tower, a free upgrade, or a relic. Win wave 12 to finish the run, then Endless.
Enemies: Runner (fast), Grunt, Shielded (armour — Sling damage halved), Swarm (many tiny), Flyer (wave 6+, only Sling + Spark can hit, ignores Frost), Brute boss every 5th wave (leak costs 3 hearts).
Keys: 1/2/3 pick hand tiles, Q/W/E/R pick towers, Space starts the wave or toggles speed, Esc pauses, M mutes.
