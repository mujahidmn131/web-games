How to Play

1. Tap an arrow tile — your cube slides in that direction until a wall, stone or locked gate stops it.
2. Arrows TURN! Every arrow you slide over rotates 90° clockwise. Tap it again later for its new direction.
3. Reach the glowing EXIT before your moves or your time run out.
Stone never moves — use it as a brake. Ice slides away when you shove it. Portals teleport you. Keys open locks.
Hint (50 coins) lights up the next best arrow. Skip (100 coins) opens the next level.
Keyboard: arrows / WASD move the cursor, Enter taps, P pauses, R restarts, H hints, M mutes.
