SPROCKET SORT

Flip the diverter — sort every gear into its matching machine!

Best score: 0 0 Level: 1

How to Play

1. Watch the belts. Gears roll down from the top — round (amber), square (blue), hex (coral).
2. Flip the diverter. Tap the lever under a belt to cycle it LEFT → STRAIGHT → RIGHT, or tap one of the three coloured chute arrows to set it directly. Each arrow matches a machine by colour.
3. Match the machine. Round gear → Round Press, square gear → Square Press, hex gear → Hex Press. A wrong machine costs 1 life and resets your combo.
4. Beat the quota. Sort the quota of gears before the timer runs out. Golden gears (level 7+) are worth 3× points; Express gears (level 12+) move 40% faster. Every 5th level is RUSH: quota +25%, timer −20%, score ×2.
Keys: 1/2/3 flip diverters 1/2/3, Space pauses, M mutes.
